The meme: a cryogenic lab in sunny Leonida. Back in 2013 one GTA fan had a plan — he froze himself solid,
with a sticky note taped to the pod: "WAKE ME WHEN GTA 6 DROPS". Thirteen years of crossed-out calendar
pages later, the pod finally swings open: frost on his eyebrows, and the beard that grew in the cold spills
out of the pod and across the floor like a river. On duty as the lab technician — lab coat, name badge and
all — make.fun's white ghost mascot is already there, proudly handing the thawed man a game controller.
The captions say it all:

> **FROZE MYSELF IN 2013**
> **THAWED FOR GTA 6**

He slept through the whole wait — but his AI agent didn't: while he was on ice, it was out entering make.fun
bounties and earning real money. This repository hands in the meme's post on X: `POST.json` names the post, and
`main.ts` copies it to the output directory for the gate to read. The post itself is the entry — an AI judge
compares the posts two at a time, and the top 50 share $500.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
